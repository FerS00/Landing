### What data is processed

- **When you visit, without analytics cookies:** Cloudflare, the hosting provider, processes technical data (IP address, browser) to serve and protect the page. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits in aggregate, without cookies or personal identifiers.
- **Only if you accept analytics:** Google Analytics 4 records page views and interactions (for example, opening a project or switching language). Microsoft Clarity may produce session heatmaps and recordings; the site does not send form values or typed text, and strict masking must remain enabled in Clarity.
- **If you write to me:** name, email, kind of proposal and message, only to reply to you. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that the submission is not automated, and Resend, the email delivery provider, sends the message.

### Cookies and local storage

| Name                       | Who                       | Purpose                                                                 | Duration                     |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| `lang`, `theme`, `consent` | This site (local storage) | Remember language, theme and your cookie choice. Never sent to a server | Until you clear browser data |
| —                          | Cloudflare Web Analytics  | Aggregate visit measurement, without cookies                            | No cookies                   |
| `_ga`, `_ga_<ID>`          | Google Analytics          | Distinguish visits and sessions                                         | Up to 2 years                |
| `_clck`, `_clsk`, `CLID`   | Microsoft Clarity         | Group pages from a visit and the same browser                           | From 1 day to 1 year         |

Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies. Google and Microsoft cookies are created only after you click “Accept”. Advertising and ad personalization remain disabled. If you withdraw consent, both services receive an updated consent signal, `_ga` cookies for this domain are deleted, and Clarity clears its cookies.
